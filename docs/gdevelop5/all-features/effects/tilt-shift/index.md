#  Tilt shift effect

Add blur to the top and bottom of the entire layer or object, leaving a sharp band in the middle. Perfect to simulate a tiny world (miniature) or a camera focus effect.

![](tilt-shift-effect.png)

The **Blur** property controls how strong the out-of-focus blur is, while the **Gradient blur** controls how gradually the image transitions from sharp to blurred — a smaller value gives a narrower in-focus band.
