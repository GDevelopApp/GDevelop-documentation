#  Tilt shift effect

Add blur to the top and bottom of the entire layer or object. Perfect to simulate a tiny world or a camera focus effect.

![](tilt-shift-effect.png)

The middle of the screen stays sharp while the top and bottom become blurred. The *Blur* setting controls how strong the blur is, and the *Gradient blur* controls how wide the sharp band in the middle is (a smaller value keeps only a thin strip in focus).
