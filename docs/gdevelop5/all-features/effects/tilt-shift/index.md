#  Tilt shift effect

Add blur to the top and bottom of the entire layer or object. Perfect to simulate a tiny world or a camera focus effect.

![](tilt-shift-effect.png)

## Parameters

- **Blur**: the strength of the blur applied to the top and bottom areas.
- **Gradient blur**: the size of the transition between the sharp central band and the blurred areas. A larger value gives a smoother, more gradual falloff; a smaller value keeps the in-focus band narrow and the blur abrupt.
