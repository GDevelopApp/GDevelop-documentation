#  Tilt shift effect

Add blur to the top and bottom of the entire layer or object. Perfect to simulate a tiny world or a camera focus effect.

![](tilt-shift-effect.png)

The **Blur** property sets how strong the blur is at the top and bottom, while the **Gradient blur** controls how gradually the image goes from sharp (in the middle) to blurred (towards the edges). A larger gradient blur keeps a wider band in focus.
