#  CRT effect

Applies a CRT effect, simulating an old cathode-ray tube television. It's most often applied to a whole layer to give a retro, arcade-cabinet feeling to the entire game.

![](crt-effect.png)

The effect combines several looks that you can mix and match: scanlines (**Line width** and **Line contrast**), screen **Curvature**, a darkened **Vignetting** around the edges and static **Noise**. The **Interlaced Lines Speed** animates the scanlines (set it to `0` to freeze them), and **Noise Frequency** controls how often the static is refreshed.
