#  God rays effect

Cast rays of light from the top of the screen. **This won't work well if shown on top of the background color of the scene**. Be sure to use an object acting as a background or a floor.

![](godray-effect.gif)

**Angle** tilts the direction of the rays, and **Center X**/**Center Y** move the point they emanate from. **Gain** and **Light** control the brightness and spread of the rays, while **Lacunarity** changes how detailed and broken up they look.

The rays are animated over time: use **Animation speed** to set how fast they shift (`0` freezes them, `1` is normal speed). Enable **Parallel** for straight, parallel rays (like distant sunlight) or disable it to make them fan out from the center point.
