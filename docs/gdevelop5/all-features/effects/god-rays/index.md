#  God rays effect

Cast rays of light from the top of the screen. **This won't work well if shown on top of the background color of the scene**. Be sure to use an object acting as a background or a floor.

![](godray-effect.gif)

The rays are animated automatically. The **Animation Speed** sets how fast they shimmer (set it to 0 to freeze them). The **Angle** rotates the direction of the rays, and **Center X**/**Center Y** move the point they appear to come from. **Gain** and **Lacunarity** change how dense and detailed the beams look. Enable **Parallel** for evenly spaced rays (like distant sunlight), or disable it to make the rays spread out from the center point.
